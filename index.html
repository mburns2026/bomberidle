WHITEPAPER

Version 0.1 — project summary.

1. Vision

Combine the addictive gameplay of an arena snake game with idle mechanics, where progress continues while the player is away, using Solana as the layer for ownership and rewards.

2. Game mechanics
  • Collecting coins grows your snake and earns $SLITHERI.
  • Eliminating rivals gives a bonus and drops coins.
  • The Auto-Bot plays by itself and drains battery; recharging costs $SLITHERI.
  • Hold left click to boost: short bursts are free; longer boosts permanently burn tSLITHERI and a little length.
3. Sustainable economy

There is no new emission: rewards come from a finite pool of 100M $SLITHERI (10M reserved for the Season 0 airdrop, 90M for daily rewards). Each day releases at most 0.1% of what remains, split among players by score, so the cap does not depend on how many players or bots exist. Shop purchases burn 50% and return 50% to the pool; on-chain withdrawals burn 5%. As a result the pool only shrinks if emission exceeds what is recycled, and total supply only decreases. No model guarantees a price: value depends on real demand for the game.

4. Security & anti-fraud

Balances will only be valid when computed on the server, never in the browser. The Phantom wallet is used only to identify the player (no permission to move funds) and, later, to sign login messages.

5. Current status

The game is a demo: the displayed balance is not on-chain and has no financial value.

6. Risks & legal notice

Crypto assets are volatile and can lose all value. This project promises no returns, is not investment advice and depends on each country's laws. Do your own research and only use money you can afford to lose.
